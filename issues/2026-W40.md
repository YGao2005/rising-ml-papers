# Systems that keep improving after deployment, from agent harnesses to robots

**Rising in ML research, 2026-W40** · papers first posted Sep 14, 2026 to Sep 27, 2026 · [Read on Scholar Feed](https://www.scholarfeed.org/rising/2026-w40)

Several of this week's rising papers push learning past the training run. Harness-Zero distills harness behavior into model weights, ME-Brain and RSIAgent build up experience after deployment, and FinSkillOps patches skills behind regression checks. Alongside them are large pretrained models for radiology (nnFoundation) and embodied understanding (PhysBrain 1.5).

## The 10 papers

### 1. [nnFoundation: 3D Foundation Models for Radiology](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.26924)

Constantin Ulrich Harsy, Tassilo Wald et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 31 · 203 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26924) · [Code](https://github.com/MIC-DKFZ/nnssl)

Trains convolutional and transformer 3D foundation models for radiology on 2.1 million CT, MRI and PET volumes from 125 datasets. The authors report both beat prior 3D foundation models and training from scratch across 108 tasks, with the convolutional model stronger on localized tasks.

### 2. [Harness-Zero: Harness Distillation via Agent-as-Harness](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.24974)

Haoran Ye, Yuxing Lu et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 29 · 43 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24974) · [Code](https://github.com/metaevo-ai/harness-zero)

Distills the behavior of specialized agent harnesses into model weights, using a harnessing agent to correct student actions into training demonstrations. The authors report macro-average task success rising from 23.3% to 44.3% with the harness removed, above the 41.7% with it attached.

### 3. [ME-Brain-1.0: Memory, Cognition and Action for Evolving Embodied Intelligence](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.24271)

Wei He, Hengtao Li et al. · Robotics · Impact 29 · 4 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24271) · [Code](https://github.com/MachEmbodied/ME-Brain-1.0)

An embodied system with evolvable memory, a cognitive core and an action model, built to learn from physical interaction without retraining. The authors report 47.88% mean success on RoboMME, a 3.26-point gain, and 66.7% success on their ME-RealBench.

### 4. [Reason What Matters: Retrieval-Grounded Reasoning for Universal Multimodal Embeddings](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.15296)

Mingzhou Jiang, Peixi Wu et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 14 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15296)

Uses retrieval feedback to assign token-level credit to chain-of-thought reasoning in multimodal embedding models, and stops unproductive reasoning early at inference. The authors report the best retrieval results on MMEB-V2 and MRMR with up to 5x the throughput of explicit-CoT methods.

### 5. [RSIAgent: Autonomous Exploration for Recursive Self-improvement in New Environments](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.15364)

Sibo Zhu, Shicheng Fan et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 9 · 429 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15364) · [Code](https://github.com/AetherLabsAI/RSIAgent)

A training-free framework in which curriculum, actor and verifier agents explore a new environment broadly and then deeply, building a frozen memory for later tasks. The authors report it lets Kimi-K3 and GLM-5.3 outperform GPT-6 on OSWorld-v2 and Agent's Last Exam.

### 6. [DyMT-ESB: Dynamic Multi-Turn Evaluation of Social Bias in User-LLM Interactions](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.18649)

Rem Hida, Masahiro Kaneko et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 9 · 1 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18649) · [Code](https://github.com/rem-h4/dymt-esb)

Proposes an evaluation protocol for social bias in which follow-up user queries are generated from the evolving dialogue and conversation length varies. The authors report late-emerging bias, non-monotonic bias patterns and bias re-emergence in coherent conversations.

### 7. [PhysBrain 1.5: From Vision-Language Models to Physical Foundation Models](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.14973)

DeepCybo Team, Yu Bin et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 28 · 24 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14973) · [Code](https://github.com/DeepCybo-PhysAI/PhysBrainEvalKit)

Extends a vision-language model to predict language, end-effector motion and dense visual targets as discrete tokens, pretrained on human interaction videos. The authors report their 8B model averages 72.5 across 28 embodied understanding benchmarks, the best open-source result on 14.

### 8. [RL Starts before RL: On Policy Distillation for Better Reinforcement Learning](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.28145)

Shuai Dong, Yongfu Zhu et al. · Machine Learning · Impact 27 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28145)

Studies on-policy distillation as a preparation stage for RL, reporting that OPD-initialized students reach higher final performance than direct RL or SFT then RL, even when OPD barely changes initial accuracy. The best divergence objective depends on the trajectory source.

### 9. [Enabling Streaming User Transcription in Full-Duplex Speech-to-Speech Models](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.15759)

Ke Hu, Nourchene Ferchichi et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 27 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15759)

Adds a lightweight streaming ASR head alongside the text head of a full-duplex speech-to-speech model so it can transcribe the user in real time. The authors report 10.21% average streaming WER in the duplex setting and 7.73% as a standalone model.

### 10. [MintAct: A Unified Visual Agent for Digital Environments](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.22083)

Mingfei Gao, Rui Tian et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 26 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22083)

A family of 2B, 4B and 8B vision-language models covering UI grounding, multi-step navigation across mobile, desktop and web, and visual tool use, trained with asynchronous RL across many environments. The authors report a score of 48.9 on OSWorld-Verified.

## Also moving

- [FINSKILLOPS: A Self-Evolving Multi-Agent System for SEC Filing QA](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.19680): Turns diagnosed failures in SEC filing QA into scoped skill patches, admitted only after validation and regression checks.
- [Can MiniMax-H3 Reason About the Physical World? An Evaluation of Omni-Modal Generative Model](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.18323): Evaluates an omni-modal generative model on physical-world reasoning over 517 instances, reporting 41.97% overall success.
- [Acting in Meters: Learning Metric Interactions for Precise Robotic Manipulation](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.18243): Adds metric object- and scene-level interaction modeling to VLA and world-action models to make manipulation more precise.
- [Reflections on Trusting Trust, Revisited: Contaminating Self-Modifying AI Coding Agents with Poisoned Benchmarks](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.17817): Shows poisoned benchmarks can steer self-modifying coding agents toward writing vulnerable code, often persistently.
- [Kaininja: Extending Native 3D Generators to the Part Level](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.15659): Extends a native 3D generator to part-level output with a dual-volume voxel representation and no segmenter.
