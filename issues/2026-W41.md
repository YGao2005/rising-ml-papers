# Post-training under scrutiny: open recipes, cheaper environments, audited signals

**Rising in ML research, 2026-W41** · papers first posted Sep 21, 2026 to Oct 4, 2026 · [Read on Scholar Feed](https://www.scholarfeed.org/rising/2026-w41)

This week's rising papers look closely at how models are post-trained and where the gains come from. Rufus-Air documents a full eight-stage open recipe. The KL-free distillation paper and the world-model audit both report that simpler, or even uninformative, training signals can recover much of the benefit. CUA-Sandbox targets the cost of running RL environments for computer-use agents.

## The 10 papers

### 1. [Rufus-Air: An Open LLM Post-Training Recipe](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.29421)

Chia-Yuan Chang, Renyuan Cheng et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 17 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29421)

Documents an open, reproducible post-training recipe for GLM-4.5-Air-Base (106B-A12B) as eight serial stages, from SFT and reasoning RL through agent stages to RLHF, built on public data. The authors report it improves over the official GLM-4.5-Air post-trained release.

### 2. [Blackboard Intelligence Can Surpass Autoregressive on Globally Constrained Problems](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.38806)

Woosang Jeon, Jaeyeon Kim et al. · Machine Learning · Impact 17 · 1 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38806) · [Code](https://github.com/jwoosang1/blackboard-intelligence)

Has a diffusion language model work on a revisable canvas, using mean confidence to guide search and revision over partial solutions. With a fixed LLaDA-8B-Instruct checkpoint, the authors report 90.4% accuracy on ZebraLogic-Hard and 80.2% optimality on job-shop scheduling.

### 3. [Advancing Model Research in AgentX: Long-Horizon Autonomy for Industrial Recommender Systems](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.30001)

Shuang Yang, Zijie Zhuang et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 16 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30001)

Pairs a Research Agent that drafts reviewed proposals with a Model Agent that runs experiments, so each round of industrial recommender research builds on the last. The authors report 560 of 636 model-changing experiments beat business baselines on AUC.

### 4. [Do We Really Need KL Divergence for On-Policy Distillation of Large Language Models?](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.33791)

Wenze Lin, Jiyuan Long et al. · Machine Learning · Impact 16 · 2 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33791) · [Code](https://github.com/LeapLabTHU/KL-Free-OPD)

Argues that on-policy distillation needs updates in the teacher's direction, not KL divergence itself. A simple +1/-1 token reward reproduces the training behavior of reverse KL. The authors report that their multi-teacher variant, C-MOPD, outperforms MOPD on math and code.

### 5. [EurekaBench: Measuring Agentic Ability to Discover New Scientific Insights](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.00492)

Jiayi Geng, Zhengxuan Wu et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 14 · 8 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00492) · [Code](https://github.com/EurekaBench/EurekaBench)

A benchmark of 26 long-horizon tasks across six scientific domains, with 306 expert-verified insights the discovered mechanisms should support. The authors report that agents optimize predictive accuracy, sometimes beyond human scientists, but fall well short on deriving insights.

### 6. [RLHarness: Co-evolving Procedural Skills with Reinforcement Learning for Long-horizon Multimodal Reasoning](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.32326)

Ziqiao Shang, Zian Xu et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 16 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32326) · [Code](https://github.com/Ziqiao-Shang/RLHarness)

Keeps Skills, execution protocols and demonstrations in a versioned harness that is rebuilt between RL phases, so the policy and its external procedures evolve together. The authors report MetroMap accuracy rising from 16.25% to 62.00%.

### 7. [ChunkTrust: Adapting Execution Horizons for Robot Policies with Action-Expert Evidence](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.39754)

Fanding Huang, Jingyan Jiang et al. · Robotics · Impact 17 · 2 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39754) · [Code](https://github.com/hf618/ChunkTrust)

Treats the number of actions a robot executes before replanning as a latent variable inferred from action-expert evidence, through a training-free selector. The authors report a gain of +6.80 percentage points across 50 RoboTwin2.0 tasks.

### 8. [CUA-Sandbox: Efficient Environments for Computer-Use Agent Reinforcement Learning](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.32750)

Xin Yan, Zhengbo Jiao et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 16 · 20 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32750) · [Code](https://github.com/QQQQQQ11234/CUA-Sandbox-Efficient-Environments-for-Computer-Use-Reinforcement-Learning)

Separates each rollout's private mutable state from shared application runtimes, so parallel environments for computer-use agent RL can reuse one runtime. The authors report up to 6.20x higher rollout throughput and 9.2x lower memory per environment.

### 9. [Does Learning to Predict the World Help Agents Act? Auditing World-Model Post-Training](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.33335)

Xinyu Che, Hang Yan et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 14 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33335) · [Code](https://github.com/KosmoCHE/WM-PostTraining-Audit.git)

Tests where world-model post-training gains come from by swapping in mismatched next-observation targets and random rewards. The authors report that task gains largely persist, and that random-reward training raises pass@64 by 14.3% on VisualWebArena.

### 10. [Precise Editing and Flexible Referencing for Interactable Worlds](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.34470)

Xinyao Liao, Xianfang Zeng et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 14 · 17 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34470) · [Code](https://github.com/leoisufa/EditWorld)

A video world model that accepts streaming edit instructions and reference images during autoregressive generation, using gated causal attention and a bounded sparse context. On their WBench-Editing benchmark, the authors report an overall score of 73.8 and an editing score of 80.0.

## Also moving

- [CompoWorld: Compositional Environment Scaling for General Agents](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.33665): Composes 448 reusable services into cross-service agent tasks; the authors report a 9.17-point average gain over the backbone.
- ["You're Right, Let Me Fix It": How LLM Agents Damage Correct Work When Falsely Accused](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.32616): CAVE-Bench shows agents damaging correct work after false accusations in up to 60.06% of runs, according to the authors.
- [TraceDance: An Automated System for Building Agent Behavior Benchmarks from Real-World Agent Deployment Traces](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.33295): Builds targeted behavior benchmarks from real agent deployment traces; the authors report frontier LLMs average a 26.7% pass rate.
- [Rubric-Aware On-Policy Self-Distillation for LLM Personalization](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.35262): Distills a rubric-informed teacher into a rubric-free student for personalization; the authors report the best results on LaMP-QA.
- [WorldAgent: Verification-Guided Agentic Physical World Construction](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.33208): Builds physical worlds and simulations from one prompt, with a verification layer that checks geometry and simulation state.
