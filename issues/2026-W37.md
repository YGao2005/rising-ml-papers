# Harder benchmarks, and the harnesses that run agents against them

**Rising in ML research, 2026-W37** · papers first posted Aug 24, 2026 to Sep 6, 2026 · [Read on Scholar Feed](https://www.scholarfeed.org/rising/2026-w37)

Much of this week's momentum is in evaluation. The Last Translation Benchmark, CultureConverse, Harbor-Index and VBVR-Pro each propose harder or more verifiable tests. Bioinfoysis and MetaRSI treat the harness around a model as something to design and improve in its own right.

## The 10 papers

### 1. [Last Translation Benchmark](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.04173)

Vilém Zouhar, Niyati Bafna et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 25 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04173)

A live, human-authored and peer-reviewed set of text, image, audio and video examples that break leading machine translation models. Each example carries handcrafted verification rules describing concrete failure cases, which the authors propose as a more actionable alternative to automatic metrics.

### 2. [CultureConverse: A Multilingual Multi-turn Simulation Harness for Culturally Grounded Assistance in East and Southeast Asia](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2608.28405)

Bryan Chen Zhengyu Tan, Weihua Zheng et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 18 · 3 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28405) · [Code](https://github.com/Social-AI-Studio/CultureConverse)

A multilingual, multi-turn simulation harness in which assistants must infer cultural constraints from partial information, covering 10 East and Southeast Asian regions, 58 subgroup identities and 7 domains. Across 18 models, the authors report GPT-5 mini gives the highest assistance quality.

### 3. [Harbor Adapters and Harbor-Index: Infrastructure and a Curated Meta-Dataset for Large-Scale Agentic Evaluation](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.04298)

Lin Shi, Haowei Lin et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 21 · 5,617 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04298) · [Code](https://github.com/harbor-framework/harbor)

Adapters that port more than 80 agentic benchmarks to one infrastructure for evaluating arbitrary agents, plus an evaluation of 8 models across 54 benchmarks. On the curated 82-task Harbor-Index, the authors report the strongest configuration, GPT-5.5 with Codex, reaches a 28.0% pass rate.

### 4. [VBVR-Pro: A Scalable and Verifiable Suite for Native Visual Reasoning](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2608.26105)

Junxiang Xu, Ruisi Wang et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 14 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26105)

A closed-loop testbed of 300 procedurally generated tasks for visual reasoning done through generation, with deterministic, task-specific reward scorers in place of VLM judges. The authors report that models trained on it transfer across seven external visual reasoning benchmarks.

### 5. [Bioinfoysis Technical Report](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.03871)

Qingyang Shao, Xin Zhang et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 20 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03871)

A multi-agent harness for long-horizon bioinformatics that treats each request as a persistent analysis run, with checklist-based replanning and validation of generated artifacts. The authors report 82.4% accuracy on BixBench and an average rise from 27.81% to 64.13% on SeqQA2.

### 6. [MetaRSI / RSI2: A Meta-Recursive Self-Improving System for Recursive Self-Improving Systems Themselves](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.06396)

Zihan Tan, Leixin Sun et al. · Machine Learning · Impact 19 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06396)

A framework for recursive self-improvement that composes three operators acting on data, the scaffold and model weights within one shared loop, scheduled by a two-level optimizer. The authors validate it on code and closed-form science with no external teacher, the target model playing every role.

### 7. [Efficient Test-Time Adaptation through Human-AI Interaction](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.04141)

Zora Zhiruo Wang, Apurva Gandhi et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 18 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04141)

A method for adapting agents to individual users from cross-session interaction data, feeding these signals into agent context and weights through an evolving rubric module. Across 30 people and 600 writing and visual creation tasks, the authors report solo task success gains of 4.5-20.9%.

### 8. [Agents in the Large: Perception-Centered Architecture for Persistent Agents](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2608.30478)

Shihan Dou, Haoxiang Jia et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 10 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30478)

A conceptual architecture for persistent language agents built around perception and control components that turn signals from task executions, internal context and the environment into lifecycle tasks. The authors use it to organize recent work and walk through a case study.

### 9. [GE-Act 2.0: Pretraining and Scaling a World-Action Model for Robotic Manipulation](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.05588)

AgiBot Research Team, Renhang Liu et al. · Robotics · Impact 20 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05588)

A world-action model for robotic manipulation whose generative and action components are trained from scratch on manipulation data. The authors report that scaling co-training data from 300 to 30,000 hours raises zero-shot success from 17.1% to 44.1% on G1-OP.

### 10. [Code as Worlds: Agentic Discovery of Executable World Representations for Physical Reasoning](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2608.27549)

Hanyang Wang, Yimo Cai et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 9 · 460 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27549) · [Code](https://github.com/mirros-lab/code-as-world)

Represents physical scenes as executable code, built by an agent that proposes, executes, renders, verifies and refines world hypotheses from text or video. The verified worlds supply training signal for a vision-language model that the authors report is state of the art on QuantiPhy.

## Also moving

- [A.X K2 Technical Report](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2608.30181): A 688B-parameter MoE model trained on 8.5T tokens; the authors report a RULER score of 94.6 out to 256K context.
- [Unsupervised Post-Training of Foundation Models: A Survey](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2608.24982): Surveys 80 methods that post-train foundation models on unlabeled inputs, grouped by the source of the update signal.
- [Apodex 1.1: Scaling Agentic Intelligence for Complex Work](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2608.23283): Scales executable environments and multi-agent coordination training for long-horizon professional work, including a 35B Mini model.
- [UI-Venus-2 Technical Report](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.00028): A GUI agent for mobile, web and desktop, trained on more than 170 multilingual mobile apps with multi-evaluator reward checks.
- [OmniPhys: A Unified Multimodal Benchmark for Physics Understanding and Generation from Chinese Educational Corpora](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2608.25398): A physics benchmark of 15,246 questions and 19,850 images from Chinese educational material that includes diagram generation.
