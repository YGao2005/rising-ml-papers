# Multilingual models, and agents aimed at science, labs and robots

**Rising in ML research, 2026-W47** · papers first posted Nov 2, 2026 to Nov 15, 2026 · [Read on Scholar Feed](https://www.scholarfeed.org/rising/2026-w47)

Two strands lead the papers gaining momentum over the past two weeks. Lara Mini and Hy ASR extend model coverage across languages and dialects, while Sage-Taiyi, the Lean quantum-program work and benchmarks such as Embodied First Exam and BEAKER put agents to work on research, robot control and lab execution. Separately, the 801-30B paper brings recursive self-improvement into a frontier pre-training run.

## The 10 papers

### 1. [Lara Mini Base: Language Diversity as a Resource, Not a Trade-off](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.16956)

Translated, Maria Pia Adinolfi et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 22 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16956)

Trains a mixture-of-experts model (7.2B total, 0.6B active parameters) from scratch on 6T tokens in English and 44 other languages. The authors report the highest average score among open base models up to 2B active parameters, and that the MoE design reduces capacity competition between languages.

### 2. [Sage-Taiyi: A Metacognitive Architecture for Scientific Discovery](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.12762)

Weida Wang, Benteng Chen et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 23 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12762)

A metacognition-inspired architecture that monitors its understanding of evidence, assumptions and open questions to choose what to investigate next and how. The authors report first place on all eight Taiyi-Bench metrics, with an Overall score of 54.0 against 47.9 for AI Scientist-v2.

### 3. [Hy ASR 3.0 Technical Report](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.14908)

Aoxiong Yin, Auden Li et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 18 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14908)

An LLM-based speech recognizer for Mandarin, English and 13 Chinese dialect groups that can use hotword lists, question-answer dialogues or summaries as context. The authors report the lowest aggregate error rates among evaluated systems on public Mandarin, English and Cantonese benchmarks.

### 4. [No Second Run: Does RSI Stop at Frontier Pre-training?](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.10719)

Xiaojie Cai, Jiayi Chen et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 20 · 76 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10719) · [Code](https://github.com/GAIR-NLP/801-30B)

Applies recursive self-improvement to a frontier pre-training run by decomposing it into smaller repeatable loops, yielding a 30B-A3B MoE base model trained from scratch on 11.8T tokens. The authors report it outperforms Qwen3-30B-A3B and Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B as a base model.

### 5. [Embodied First Exam](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.12177)

Kangrui Wang, Qineng Wang et al. · Robotics · Impact 19 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12177)

A benchmark of 1,156 tasks in 35 suites on 47 robots in which coding agents choose to control robots directly, write control code, or train a policy. On the 456 core tasks, the authors report the strongest of eight agents, GPT-6 Astra in Codex, reaches 34% success in the Standard Setting.

### 6. [WorldFoundry: Towards Unified Infrastructure for World Intelligence](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.10771)

Juanxi Tian, Jiadong Hou et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 16 · 352 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10771) · [Code](https://github.com/OpenEnvision/WorldFoundry)

Defines a generative world model by persistent, action-responsive state and presents open-source infrastructure for reproducible inference and evaluation across video, 3D/4D, embodied and interactive systems. The catalog holds 270 model entries, 74 benchmarks and 62 registered metrics.

### 7. [Discovering formally verified quantum programs with AI](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.09822)

Sergey Shirobokov, Ben Jaderberg et al. · Quantum Physics · Impact 17 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09822)

Builds a quantum program library in Lean where teams of LLM agents synthesize programs against formal specifications and verify their correctness and cost. The authors report improvements over prior baselines of 290x for quantum arithmetic and 11x for simulations relevant to fusion.

### 8. [Micius-Flash: Lightweight Agentic Intelligence for Engineering](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.10015)

Hongcheng Gao, Weiheng Li et al. · Software Engineering · Impact 17 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10015)

A 27B-parameter multimodal engineering model with a 256K-token context, trained by merging expert models through multi-teacher on-policy distillation. The authors report 64.2% on hardware code benchmarks, 15.0 points above a same-size baseline, and 62.4% on a semiconductor agent benchmark.

### 9. [BEAKER: An Expert-Curated Benchmark for Embodied Brains in Self-Driving Chemical Laboratories](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.06911)

Fei Lin, Tengchao Zhang et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 16 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06911)

A benchmark of 1,000 expert-curated samples across 11 image and video task types for multimodal models in self-driving chemical labs. Testing 28 models, the authors find they follow coarse procedures but struggle to ground actions, recognize fine-grained apparatus and safety norms, and order steps.

### 10. [MIRIX-2.0: Multi-Agent Memory System](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.12068)

Wei-Chieh Huang, Weizhi Zhang et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 14 · 3,464 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12068) · [Code](https://github.com/Mirix-AI/MIRIX)

A memory system for LLM agents in which six typed memory agents keep complete records, indexed by a provenance hypergraph and retrieved under explicit budgets. The authors report it is the most accurate of the compared systems on four of five QA benchmarks, including 91.4% on LoCoMo.

## Also moving

- [Infrathon: Benchmarking Language Agents for Real-World, Long-Horizon AI Infra Optimization](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.11995): Benchmarks frontier agents on long-horizon AI infrastructure optimization and reports that model rankings shift with the horizon.
- [Learning When to Think: Dual-Reference Offline Optimization for Adaptive VLM Reasoning](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.09154): Teaches a vision-language model when to answer directly and when to reason step by step, using a dual-reference offline objective.
- [Compass 1: Towards Open-World and Persistent Embodied Navigation from Monocular RGB Videos](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.12087): A navigation model that draws on long visual history from monocular RGB video; the authors report 79.4% overall success.
- [SpecOPD: Branch-Parallel On-Policy Distillation for Speculative Decoding](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.02846): Branch-parallel on-policy distillation for speculative-decoding drafters; the authors report up to 3.56× generation speedup.
- [Measuring AI Agents as Teammates with Collaborative Intelligence](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.02633): A 240-task benchmark of how well AI agents work as teammates; the authors report the best of 18 models scores 82.2 out of 100.
