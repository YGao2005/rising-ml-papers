# Recursive self-improvement and synthetic training loops for agents

**Rising in ML research, 2026-W38** · papers first posted Aug 31, 2026 to Sep 13, 2026 · [Read on Scholar Feed](https://www.scholarfeed.org/rising/2026-w38)

Two papers this week address recursive self-improvement directly. NeoHorse-1 builds a post-training loop driven by routing, and The Last AI Built by Humans sets out a roadmap. Close by, FrogNano trains a small coding agent on synthesized tasks, Terminal-Universe rebuilds training environments from agent trajectories, and S3Gym tests whether models can improve from their own experience.

## The 10 papers

### 1. [NeoHorse-1: Towards Recursive Self-Improvement via Agentic Post-Training with Routing Harness](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.08183)

NeoHorse Team, Guoliang Cao et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 19 · 1,054 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08183) · [Code](https://github.com/TokenRhythm/NeoHorse)

Routes user interactions through a heterogeneous model pool and turns the logged records into data for a three-stage fine-tuning curriculum and on-policy distillation. Across eleven benchmarks, the authors report macro-averages rising from 58.94 to 64.87 at 4B and from 65.60 to 69.04 at 9B.

### 2. [The Last AI Built by Humans: Toward Genuine Recursive Self-Improvement](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.11873)

Yi Duan, Ying Liu et al. · Machine Learning · Impact 20 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11873)

A position and roadmap paper on recursive self-improvement. It stages autonomy levels from improvement execution up to recursive meta-improvement, uses a Headroom-Closed Index to point out problems in existing LLMs, and examines scenarios such as scientific discovery and software engineering.

### 3. [SenseNova-U1.5: Towards Native Unified Visual Intelligence](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.11929)

Haiwen Diao, Jiahao Wang et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 17 · 6,891 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11929) · [Code](https://github.com/OpenSenseNova/SenseNova-U1)

An 8B-MoT unified multimodal model that understands and generates images without an encoder or VAE. Specialist experts are merged through multi-expert on-policy distillation. The authors report gains in image fidelity, text rendering and multi-reference editing, at native resolutions up to 4K.

### 4. [Omni Interaction Agent Technical Report](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.08977)

Orantqing, Shengpeng Ji et al. · Audio & Speech Processing · Impact 17 · 298 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08977) · [Code](https://github.com/Omni-Interaction-Gander/Omni-Interaction-Agent)

Gander is an end-to-end model for full-duplex interaction over streaming video, speech and text. It pairs a real-time Cerebellum with a reasoning Brain. The authors' internal human evaluations report spoken dialogue on par with leading open models and competitive omni interaction.

### 5. [FrogNano: Training a 4B Coding Agent via Online Task Synthesis](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.07925)

Minseon Kim, Zhengyan Shi et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 17 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07925)

Trains a 4B coding agent with RL alone on around 1,500 synthetic software engineering environments, with no distillation from larger models. The authors report that generating tasks at the learnability frontier of the current checkpoint is a key ingredient.

### 6. [AuK Technical Report: An Open-Source Foundational Model for Speech Generation and Editing](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.08936)

Ziyang Ma, Zhikang Niu et al. · Sound · Impact 14 · 1,271 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08936) · [Code](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/AuK)

Handles both speech generation and editing through natural-language instructions and was trained on about 3.03 billion instruction-audio instances. The authors report that the distilled AuK-Flash runs 4-step inference with a 4.5x wall-clock speedup over the full model.

### 7. [OpenWAM: An Open, Modular Exploration Towards Systematic World-Action Model Pretraining](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.07398)

Yuran Wang, Siqiao Huang et al. · Robotics · Impact 11 · 890 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07398) · [Code](https://github.com/OpenWAM-Official/OpenWAM)

Splits world-action model design into composable modules and uses controlled experiments to derive three design principles. The resulting OpenWAM-α was pretrained on about 6,400 hours of human and robot data, and the authors report strong results across eight simulation benchmarks and on real robots.

### 8. [Jina-OCR-v1: Efficient Document Parsing with Speculative Decoding and Dense Verifiable Rewards](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.03181)

Alejandro Barón García, Feng Wang et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 15 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03181)

An end-to-end document parser for low-budget GPUs. It adds a FastMTP speculative decoding head and trains with GRPO under dense verifiable rewards. The authors report 91.14 on OmniDocBench v1.6, 83.4 on olmOCR-Bench, and 2.57 pages per second.

### 9. [xDailyBench: Benchmarking LLMs on Professional Consultation for Real-Life Problems](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.07784)

Yongchang Peng, Qingshui Gu et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 16 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07784)

A benchmark of 248 real-life consultation tasks across 51 scenarios, scored with rubrics that cover explicit and implicit requirements. The authors report that the best of 11 frontier models reaches 75.6%, and that every model scores at least 9 points lower on implicit requirements.

### 10. [Causal multi-modal AI for personalized chemosensitivity prediction](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.13567)

Dhruva Biswas, Jeroen Berrevoets et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 15 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13567)

A causal multi-modal model that predicts each breast cancer patient's benefit from chemotherapy using pathology and clinical data. It was developed on 9,141 patients and evaluated on 1,994. The authors estimate it could cut chemotherapy use by 30% at the same recurrence-free rate.

## Also moving

- [NCP-ArchPreview Technical Report: Moving towards Latent Space Language Models through Next Concept Prediction](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.10715): Adds next-concept prediction to next-token pretraining and reports reaching OLMo-3-7B's final loss with 51.3% of the tokens.
- [S3Gym: Can LLMs Turn Self-Testing and Self-Judging into Self-Improvement?](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2608.31100): A benchmark of seven text-based games that tests LLM self-improvement; the authors find gains are neither automatic nor uniform.
- [Terminal-Universe: Turning Agent Trajectories into Scalable Terminal Environments](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.04148): Rebuilds executable terminal environments from existing agent trajectories, producing 37.3k environments for training code agents.
- [Mitra-v2 Technical Report](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.04540): A tabular foundation model trained only on synthetic data; reports matching a 1.6B-parameter TabFM with 77M parameters.
- [CogEvol: Towards Efficient and Reliable Learning Environment Generation](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2608.30968): Models that turn course briefs into slides or interactive HTML in one pass, with a reported median of 17 seconds per slide.
