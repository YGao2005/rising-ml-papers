# Testing agents where the work happens: repositories, robots and collaborators

**Rising in ML research, 2026-W45** · papers first posted Oct 19, 2026 to Nov 1, 2026 · [Read on Scholar Feed](https://www.scholarfeed.org/rising/2026-w45)

This week's rising papers measure agents in working conditions rather than on isolated tasks. FrontierCS 2 places agents inside research repositories, PersonaScienceBench varies the researcher they work with, and Auriga wraps a language model in a harness for robot manipulation. Alongside them, Sage-A1.5, Nemotron 3.5 and IQuest-Q1 are new agentic model reports, and Timestral brings foundation models to probabilistic forecasting.

## The 10 papers

### 1. [Timestral](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.37585)

Adrien Petralia, Alexandre Gavaudan et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 46 · 1,081 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37585) · [Code](https://github.com/mistralai/timestral)

A family of four forecasting models, 5M to 209M parameters, that separates prediction tokens from causally encoded context and pretrains only on synthetic data. The authors report the best results on GIFT-Eval, TIME and fev-bench, with the two largest models placing top two on every metric.

### 2. [Sage-A1.5: From Long-Horizon Execution to Scientific Exploration with Capability Composition](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.28578)

Wenjie Lou, Tianshuo Peng et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 46 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28578)

A 35B Mixture-of-Experts agentic model that extends A1 with subagent delegation in isolated contexts, progress checking and strategy revision, and exploratory research producing inspectable artifacts. The authors showcase it on complex agentic tasks and multidisciplinary scientific applications.

### 3. [FrontierCS 2: A Benchmark for Computer Science R&D](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.29932)

Qiuyang Mang, Wenhao Chai et al. · Machine Learning · Impact 46 · 84 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29932) · [Code](https://github.com/FrontierCS/FrontierCS-2)

A benchmark of 48 open-ended tasks across 13 domains, built by deleting a paper's contribution from its released code and asking agents to patch the repository. Of 12 agents, the authors report Claude Opus 5.5 does best, passing 58% of runs, while no run passes on 16 tasks.

### 4. [AI Cosmos: An Agentic Infra for Situated Learning](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.40031)

AI Cosmos Team, Jinfeng Zhou et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 43 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40031)

An agentic infrastructure that places learners and role-specialized agents in learning scenarios and keeps practice as reusable assets. In two preregistered randomized studies, the authors report greater learning gains in legal reasoning and higher mastery of unseen programming problems than LLM baselines.

### 5. [Nemotron 3.5 Super and Lightning: Open, Efficient Mixture-of-Experts Hybrid Mamba-Transformer Models for Agentic AI](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.38095)

NVIDIA, Aastha Jhunjhunwala et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 42 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38095)

Open Mixture-of-Experts hybrid Mamba-Transformer models: Super (120B parameters, 12B active, multimodal) and Lightning (30B, 3B active, text). The authors report Super reaches 51.78% on SWE-bench Pro, ahead of Nemotron 3 Ultra with about 4.6x fewer active parameters, and up to 4,856 output tokens/s/GPU.

### 6. [IQuest-Q1: Advancing Foundation Capabilities for Agentic CLI Systems](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.30949)

A. Jian Yang, An Huang et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 42 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30949)

An open-source sparse Mixture-of-Experts model with 320B total parameters and 15B activated, developed for agentic coding through three stages of pre-, mid- and post-training plus model merging. The abstract reports capability in coding and general tasks but no specific metrics.

### 7. [PersonaScienceBench: Evaluating Scientific Agents with Diverse Simulated Researchers](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.32106)

Siyang Wu, Weizhi Zhang et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 37 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32106)

Evaluates scientific agents working with simulated researchers whose persona profiles vary while objectives and grading stay fixed, using 200 tasks from five benchmarks across four domains. Across eighteen model configurations, the authors report that agent behaviors can differ even when final scores match.

### 8. [Auriga Technical Report: A Task-Agnostic Agent Harness for Zero-Shot Embodied Manipulation](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.01112)

Chengcheng Wang, Yuheng Shi et al. · Robotics · Impact 37 · 29 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01112) · [Code](https://github.com/lose4578/Auriga)

A task-agnostic harness in which a language model makes task decisions and acts through tool calls or Python programs, while the harness supplies 3D measurements and stops on divergence. The authors report closing 68.3% of the planning-execution gap and ranking first on RoboDojo with a 57.7% success rate.

### 9. [Venus-DefakerOne2: A Vision-Forensics Foundation Model with Broader Coverage](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.37918)

Chenfan Qu, Yachao Liang et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 35 · 69 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37918) · [Code](https://github.com/inclusionAI/DefakerOne2)

A binary forgery detector covering images, videos, documents and physical spoofs, combining alternating LoRA and full-parameter fine-tuning with entropy-based test adaptation. The authors report 96.7% on DeepFake faces, 96.0% on AI-generated videos and 87.6% on tampered documents, ahead of specialized models.

### 10. [Hy-Nomas: Towards Free-form Interactive World Generation](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.36729)

Qiaoling Chen, Jiaxiang Cheng et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 35 · 497 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36729) · [Code](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy-Nomas)

A video world model with an 8B diffusion backbone that follows free-form text and camera control across turns, restricting action instructions to their intended time windows. The authors report it beats existing world models in human evaluation and release weights, code and benchmarks.

## Also moving

- [CulturalRiddles: A Multicultural Benchmark of Riddles](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.29968): Human-authored riddle benchmark spanning 62 cultures and 51 languages; the authors report at most 22% accuracy in some communities.
- [MemT-1.0: Towards Memory-Native Evolution](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.26825): Builds memory into model computation; the authors report 1.86× faster inference and 66× less storage than full KV cache at 128K tokens.
- [Memory-Centric Continual Learning for AI Agents](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.29357): Proposes a taxonomy, evaluation protocol and scaling framework that judge agent memory by whether it improves future actions.
- [Trial by Trace: Benchmarking AI Agent Harnesses for Reliability and Failure Attribution](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.32285): Benchmarks agent harness reliability over 3,420 runs, reporting macro-averaged harness scores from 59.8% to 72.0%.
- [CompSkill: Compiling Execution Experience for Skill Retrieval](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.01115): Retrieves skill documents using compiled execution experience; the authors report SkillRet NDCG@10 rising from 64.0 to 70.9.
