# Agents tested on whole jobs, from physics research to chained coding requests

**Rising in ML research, 2026-W46** · papers first posted Oct 26, 2026 to Nov 8, 2026 · [Read on Scholar Feed](https://www.scholarfeed.org/rising/2026-w46)

This week's rising papers test agents on whole workflows instead of isolated steps. In FrontierPhysics, agents have to produce publishable research. BOBA chains coding requests together, and WebHarbor rebuilds real websites as stable training environments. A second thread is recursive self-improvement, from the generalization ladder in SelfImprove-Bench to Compounding Physical RSI on robots.

## The 10 papers

### 1. [FrontierPhysics: Evaluating Agents for End-to-End Physics Research](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.06910)

Bingran You, Wenjun Ke et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 22 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06910)

Introduces 56 research tasks written by 42 physicists across nine domains; agents must review literature, plan, run experiments and write a manuscript. Across 22 models, the authors report no configuration passes more than 46 of its 168 rollouts.

### 2. [WebHarbor: Docking Real Websites for Evolving GUI Agent Environments](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.03634)

Zhaoyang Wang, Qianhui Wu et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 21 · 62 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03634) · [Code](https://github.com/aiming-lab/WebHarbor)

Rebuilds real web services as 153 dockerized, deterministic environments with 3,518 tasks, including post-login workflows. The authors report that a Qwen 4B-level agent trained with RL in them generalizes to unseen tasks and is competitive on four benchmarks.

### 3. [SelfImprove-Bench: Evaluating Generalization in Self-Improving Agents](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.02797)

Bangrui Xu, Jie Yang et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 19 · 5 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02797) · [Code](https://github.com/theseus-labs-rsi/SelfImprove-Bench)

An evaluation framework for recursively self-improving agents, with game-based tasks grouped into Exploring, Planning and Reasoning and a G0 to G4 generalization ladder. The authors report that gains from existing methods are inconsistent across ladder levels.

### 4. [Compounding Physical RSI: Turning Execution Failure into Inherited Capability](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.06654)

Yixuan Yang, Qiang Chen et al. · Robotics · Impact 18 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06654)

A self-improving robot policy framework. It traces failed rollouts to the stage that broke, refines the policy with behavior-regularized offline RL, and protects existing skills through weight-space candidate selection. Across 42 RoboDojo-Sim tasks, the authors report a 20.70% success rate.

### 5. [EmbeddingGemma 2: Unified and Efficient Multimodal Representations](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.37364)

Henrique Schechter Vera, Sahil Dua et al. · Machine Learning · Impact 16 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37364)

An open embedding model placing text, images, audio, video and their combinations in one space. The authors report it outperforms specialist models and models two to twelve times larger, and it supports selective modality loading, truncatable dimensions and quantized weights.

### 6. [Democratizing Robot-Use Agents: A Unified Harness with Open Models](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.01560)

Jiarui Yuan, Yuxin Zuo et al. · Robotics · Impact 18 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01560)

Presents uni-robot-agent, an open-weight harness for robot-use agents that supports further training. The authors report that an out-of-the-box Qwen3.8-Flash-Next reaches a RoboDojo Score of 31.39, above the published GPT-6 Astra-RoboProbe result of 28.97. They link effective robot use to four general capabilities.

### 7. [RynnWorld-Latent: A Cross-Embodiment World Model Conditioned on Universal Latent Actions](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.36791)

Haoyu Zhao, Yinzhou Tang et al. · Robotics · Impact 17 · 16 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36791) · [Code](https://github.com/alibaba-damo-academy/RynnWorld-Latent)

A cross-embodiment video world model conditioned on 3D-grounded latent actions, so human videos, robot demos and simulation share one interface. Pretrained on about 553.5M frames from 23 datasets, it improves video prediction and policy success on LIBERO and real robots, the authors report.

### 8. [Selecting or Solving? What Best-of-N Verification Delivers in Video Reasoning](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.00507)

Martin Q. Ma, Yuxiao Qu et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 15 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00507) · [Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/best-n-selection-video-reasoning)

Separates what a verifier adds by selecting among best-of-N candidates (the Selection Increment) from what it achieves solving alone, across five long-video benchmarks. On Video-Holmes, the authors report a weak verifier gains +19.3 points yet lands 7.1 below the majority vote.

### 9. [Reviso: Not All Visual Evidence Deserves Equal Attention in Video Captioning](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.03602)

Tengfei Liu, Yang Shi et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 15 · 2 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03602) · [Code](https://github.com/pkucs-Ltf/Reviso)

An agentic video captioner that plans what to observe from a low-frame-rate preview, then revisits selected segments with tools; it is trained with GRPO using plan-alignment and tool-cost rewards. The authors report it beats evaluated open-source baselines on four benchmarks.

### 10. [Built on Broken Assumptions (BOBA): Why Coding Agents Cannot Be Left Alone](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.02207)

Alejandro Cuadron, Arpit Gupta · Software Engineering · Impact 17 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02207) · [Code](https://github.com/amazon-agi/BOBA-bench)

Builds 280 chains of 20 consecutive coding requests from 140 open-source projects. Across ten agents, the authors report completion falls from 95% on single requests to 51% on chains, and one external error signal plus a repair round lifts it to 59%.

## Also moving

- [CoworkOS:Scaling Agent Coworkers for Real-world Automation](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.03178): A three-layer harness for long-horizon cowork agents with persistent memory, shared workspaces and human-in-the-loop editing.
- [CUA-Master: Scaling Computer-use Agents through Open-World Recursive Improvements](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.03126): A nested double-loop framework that mines workflows from instructional videos to synthesize training tasks for computer-use agents.
- [LEANGUARD: Lean-Verified Guardrails for Tool-Using Agents](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.02332): Checks every agent tool call against a Lean4 policy before execution; the authors report injection success falls from 57.9% to 5.9%.
- [Encode Once, Answer Many: Internalizing Context via Parametric Flow](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.38356): Generates a LoRA adapter from a document in one forward pass, so a frozen model can answer many queries without rereading it.
- [Is Your Long-Horizon Benchmark Really Deep? Measuring and Synthesizing Depth in Terminal Tasks](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2611.01927): Measures terminal-task depth as the number of turns that must run in sequence, and synthesizes 105 deeper tasks segment by segment.
