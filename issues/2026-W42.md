# Scaling the environments, graders and harnesses that let agents keep improving

**Rising in ML research, 2026-W42** · papers first posted Sep 28, 2026 to Oct 11, 2026 · [Read on Scholar Feed](https://www.scholarfeed.org/rising/2026-w42)

This week's rising papers are about the scaffolding around models: the environments, graders and harnesses that let agents train and work over long horizons. MiMo-V2.6 scales RL compute and grading. WorkForge and AgentGarten build training environments at scale, and Stateless Language Agents moves research state out of the agent's context. New benchmarks for jailbreak robustness and persona fidelity round out the list.

## The 10 papers

### 1. [MiMo-V2.6: Scaling Reinforcement Learning Towards Self-Improvement](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.11959)

Core Team, Zongming Qiao et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 20 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11959)

An omni-modal model series trained by scaling RL compute three ways: larger asynchronous batches (1,568 samples and 2.7-3.7B tokens per step), more varied code, general, visual and cyber environments, and more grader compute via groupwise agentic grading. The authors open-source the environments and RL framework.

### 2. [Scaling Verifiable Environments for Long-horizon Work Agents](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.04906)

Jiazheng Zhang, Long Ma et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 18 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04906)

WorkForge builds verifiable work-agent environments from real-world files. It derives tasks and verifiers from checkable facts in each workspace and yields 16.7K environments across 40 domains. The authors report that post-training raised Qwen3.5-35B-A3B-Base from 45.5 to 73.6 on GDPVal and from 5.0 to 21.3 on APEX.

### 3. [TimeNet: An Extensible Unified Data Infrastructure for Next-Generation Temporal Foundation Models](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.04407)

Martin Maritsch, Timo Stoffregen et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 19 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04407)

An open data standard that stores time-series signals, metadata, annotations and task definitions in one shared model, so heterogeneous datasets can be combined for training. The authors transcode 1.5M task instances and report a 14% F1 gain from joint training over single-dataset models.

### 4. [MLCommons Jailbreak Benchmark v1.0](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.02827)

Carsten Maple, Cagatay Yucel et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 18 · 24 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02827) · [Code](https://github.com/mlcommons/jailbreak-taxonomy)

An end-to-end method for testing LLM robustness to single-turn text jailbreaks, with paired baseline and adversarial runs, human annotation and evaluator calibration. Across eight open-weight systems and 264 seed prompts, the authors report the unsafe-response rate rose from 11.08% to 18.65%.

### 5. [AgentPersonaBench: Benchmarking Persona-Driven User Simulation](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.04379)

Jintao Huang, Yifan Wang et al. · Artificial Intelligence · Impact 18 · 3 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04379) · [Code](https://github.com/MatrAIx-ai/AgentPersonaBench)

A benchmark of 2,460 tasks covering 867 traits. It checks whether persona conditioning steers what simulated users do across survey, chat, web and app surfaces. The authors report up to 84.7% full-pass adherence, but only 37.9-64.3% of cases pass all four surfaces.

### 6. [VFold: Symmetry-Aware Cross-Layer Value Cache Compression](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.12338)

Neha Verma, Sungwon Kim et al. · Computation & Language · Impact 15 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12338) · [Code](https://github.com/nverma1/vfold)

Proposes a symmetry-aware method that merges value caches across layers to cut KV-cache memory during decoding without architectural changes. The authors report no harmful performance loss and higher compression ratios when it is combined with quantization or key cache pruning.

### 7. [AgentGarten: Code Worlds for Evolving Agents](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.12374)

Jiawei Chi, Shangchen Miao et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 15 · 155 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12374) · [Code](https://github.com/MirroS-Lab/AgentGarten)

Pairs simulators and game engines with a shared neural renderer, distilled from a video model with a proposed Adversarial Forcing method, to create real-time visual environments. Agents pass playbooks forward between rounds, and the authors report learning in 4 rounds versus millions for a conventional RL counterpart.

### 8. [Stateless Language Agents: Scaling Long-Horizon Automated Research](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.07625)

Qizheng Zhang, Changxiu Ji et al. · Machine Learning · Impact 16 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07625) · [Code](https://github.com/Alex-q-z/stateless-language-agentsSLA)

An automated-research framework in which agents keep no conversation history. The harness owns the research state and builds a fresh context for each call. The authors report the best final result on every task at budgets up to one billion tokens, and matching a strong kernel baseline with over 84% fewer tokens.

### 9. [Video Prediction Policy 2: Predict Better, Act Better](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.10270)

Yanjiang Guo, Haodong Yan et al. · Computer Vision · Impact 15 · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10270)

A world action model whose video model is pretrained on manipulation videos, distilled into a single-step planner and given a mixture-of-transformers action module. The authors report VPP2-14B beats Cosmos3-64B by 11.0 points on video instruction-following and the strongest baseline by 18.5 points on ALOHA tasks.

### 10. [OpenRUA: Robot-Use Agents Are Zero-Shot Visuomotor Policies](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.02459)

Zhaoyang Chu, Earl T. Barr et al. · Robotics · Impact 17 · 5 GitHub stars · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02459) · [Code](https://github.com/terminalworld/OpenRUA)

Gives an off-the-shelf coding agent only terminal access to a robot's native ROS 2 interface, with no bespoke primitives or task-specific training. Using Claude Code, the authors report 99.0% success on CaP-Bench and 87.0% on LIBERO-PRO.

## Also moving

- [Large Language Continuous Diffusion Models](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.02665): Sigma is a 3B and 8B continuous diffusion language model, warm-started from autoregressive weights and steerable in embedding space.
- [Lend Me Your Eyes: Instruction-Aware Text Embeddings via Attention Relay](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.05564): Passes an instruction-tuned LLM's attention weights into a text embedder, making the embedder instruction-aware without training.
- [RSI-Master: Structuring Experiments to Guide Autonomous Model Improvement](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2609.35561): An agent system for autonomous post-training that regularizes experiments and organizes Workers and Reviewers in a research DAG.
- [The Persona Hierarchy Model: Understanding Contextual Generalization in Fine-Tuning LLMs](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.09384): Explains when fine-tuning generalizes across contexts via a shared default persona, and adds a regularizer that curbs reward hacking.
- [VeriFine: Scaling Verification for Self-Improvement in Embodied Reasoning](https://www.scholarfeed.org/paper/2610.08761): Co-evolves an embodied reasoning policy, its curriculum and its judge, bringing in humans when verification becomes the bottleneck.
